For a Kitgun, Zaw, or other custom build, enter each physical damage type's share of total base physical damage (Impact + Puncture + Slash). Use the unmodded weapon's Arsenal stats or its weapon entry in the Warframe Wiki. Example: 10 Impact, 20 Puncture, 70 Slash. Stats appear only when that damage type is more than 25% of the physical total; elemental damage does not count toward this split. This is an estimate if your assembled modular weapon's base stats differ from the listed component profile.
